This policy explains how No Brainers Co. collects, uses, stores, and protects data accessed through third-party APIs.

1. Who We Are

No Brainers Co. (“No Brainers”, “we”, “us”) is a digital marketing agency providing analytics-based marketing services to third-party clients. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect data accessed through third party APIs.

Contact: noclue@no-brainers.com | 100 Kings Point Dr. Unit 1223, Sunny Isles Beach, FL 33160, US | www.no-brainers.com

2. Data We Access via the API

We access only the data needed to deliver our services, and only with authorization from the data owner through the respective vendor’s OAuth process.

  • Advertising data (where authorized): campaign, audience, and performance metrics from client ad accounts.
  • Authorized account data: basic profile details (name, profile ID, headline) of the authorizing user or client administrator.
  • Organization Page data: page content, followers, and engagement metrics (impressions, clicks, reactions, comments, shares).

We do not access private messages or connections, and we do not collect data outside the permissions granted.

3. How We Use the Data

  • Operating, securing, and troubleshooting our services
  • Reporting and analytics dashboards for our clients
  • Measuring and improving the performance of client content and campaigns
  • Scheduling and managing posts on behalf of clients (where authorized)

4. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not scrape or use unauthorized means to obtain data.
  • We do not sell, rent, or license data to anyone.
  • We do not use data to train generative AI or machine learning models.
  • We do not use data for purposes other than those the data owner authorized.
  • We do not combine data with other sources to build profiles of individuals for unrelated purposes.

5. Who We Share Your Data With

  • With the client who owns the account or page the data came from.
  • With service providers (e.g., hosting, security) bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations.
  • When required by law or valid legal process.

6. Storage, Security, and Retention

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with access limited to authorized personnel. Access tokens are stored securely and never exposed in client-side code. We retain data only as long as needed to provide services, and no longer than [30 days/year] after the client relationship ends or authorization is revoked. Data is then deleted or irreversibly anonymized. We refresh or remove data in line with the vendor’s API Terms.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Revoke access: at any time via the mail provided below.
  • Access, correction, deletion: email yes-privacy@no-brainers.com and we will respond within [30] days.
  • Objection or restriction: you may request that we stop processing your data.

8. International Transfers

Data may be processed outside your country. Where we do so, we apply appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law.

9. Children

Our services are not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Compliance with API Use

Our use of data complies with all the third party API Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

11. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the revised version here with a new “Last updated” date. Material changes will be communicated to affected clients.
